Objective read (report, not spin, per the plan's instruction): f1 (the
doorway/archway view, closest camera distance to the large checkerboard-
floor polygons and stone-railed hall walls) shows by far the largest and
most widespread difference -- 90% of pixels differ, mean channel delta
11/255. This is consistent with the plan's predicted "1999-era signature":
per-vertex lighting on large, sparsely-tessellated polys (the checkerboard
floor, hall walls) produces visible banding/gradient-interpolation
artifacts across the polygon face that per-pixel shading avoids -- the
lightweight crop at f1 shows a visibly flatter, more uniformly-lit floor
with coarser light falloff versus the per-pixel crop's smoother radial
falloff around the player-light position. f0 and f2 (rooms/halls with
smaller, more numerous polys and a farther/more oblique light) show
smaller, more localized differences (~30%, mean delta 1-3/255) -- the
banding effect is present but far less visually obvious with finer
tessellation. Will may find the lightweight look period-appropriate
(Voodoo1-era EQ shipped exactly this); no acceptability judgment is made
here.
