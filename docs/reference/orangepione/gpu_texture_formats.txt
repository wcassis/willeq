=== Interpretation guide ===
  ratio < 0.7x  : Compressed upload is significantly faster than RGBA upload.
                  The GPU is decoding the format in hardware (less data to transfer,
                  no CPU decode overhead).
  ratio ~ 1.0x  : Similar speed. May be hardware-decoded but with overhead,
                  or the test is bottlenecked elsewhere. Inconclusive.
  ratio > 1.1x  : Compressed upload is slower than RGBA. The driver is
                  software-decoding on the CPU before uploading to GPU.
